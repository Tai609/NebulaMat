**Title:** Noble-metal advantage: superior sinter-resistance of Au versus Cu nanoparticle catalysts

**Type:** experimental (no calculations in the original manuscript)

## Abstract (excerpt)

Au and Cu nanoparticles (4–6 nm) were aged 10 h at 650 K and imaged by TEM.
Both coarsened, but we argue on chemical grounds that Au is the more robust
support-free catalyst. We attribute this to gold's noble character: its
filled-d, chemically inert lattice should resist point-defect (vacancy)
formation more strongly than the more reactive copper lattice.
