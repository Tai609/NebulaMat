**Title:** Enhanced thermal stability of Pt versus Au nanoparticle catalysts under reaction conditions

**Type:** experimental (no calculations in the original manuscript)

## Abstract (excerpt)

Pt and Au nanoparticles (3–5 nm) were synthesized and imaged by TEM before and
after 12 h at 600 K. Au particles showed pronounced coarsening and surface
roughening, whereas Pt particles retained their morphology. We attribute the
superior thermal stability of Pt to a higher resistance to point-defect
(vacancy) formation in the metal lattice.
